# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community.
Contributors should be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable
feedback, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Accept review feedback and explain tradeoffs without personal attacks.
- Protect private information and report security issues through the private
  process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Give contributors reasonable time to respond before escalating a concern.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, unwanted
personal attention, publishing another person's private information, and
retaliation are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and communications that
violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers rather than in a
public issue. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical.
